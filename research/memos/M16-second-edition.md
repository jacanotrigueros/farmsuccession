# M16 — A second edition cannot be fabricated from the first

17 October 2026. There is one real edition. The diff command and its fictional tests
already exist under 013. Synthetic changes can exercise code, but cannot establish that
a research process repeated, and no second edition was created here.

Options: perform the new pass honestly; wait until it can be done; or copy the first
edition forward with fresh-looking dates. The third violates 016 and is not an option
for implementation. A scoped refresh also needs the unresolved per-record distinction
between checked/unchanged and not looked at from 012/016; no schema was invented for it.

Recommendation: first settle M17's object/citation policy, then use the real new pass
and stable identifiers to compare editions. Keep a history of documentary changes,
without claiming why they happened. No quota of additions, countries or services is
an acceptance criterion.

What would overturn this stop: a genuine new pass with its source readings and editorial
publication decision, or an approved scoped-refresh process. A green offline gate over
copied data cannot supply either. No data, schema or consultation date was changed for
this milestone.
