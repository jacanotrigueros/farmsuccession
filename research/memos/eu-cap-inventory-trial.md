# EU intervention inventory: source trial

18 October 2026. Scope: the EU layer and the five countries currently in the
catalogue (ES, FR, DE, IT, PT). This is a research route, not a new published
resource or a claim of complete coverage.

## Reusable source

- [Catalogue of CAP Interventions, EU CAP Network](https://eu-cap-network.ec.europa.eu/publications/catalogue-cap-interventions_en): the Commission describes a searchable catalogue of interventions extracted from Member States' CAP Strategic Plans. Its page describes filters for Member State, intervention type and beneficiary, plus intervention design and planned allocations. Checked 2026-10-18.
- [European Commission legal notice](https://commission.europa.eu/legal-notice_en): EU-owned website content is available under CC BY 4.0 unless an individual notice says otherwise; credit and an indication of changes are required. Third-party material, marks and other excluded rights need separate clearance. Checked 2026-10-18.

## Relationship to Farm Succession

| Inventory material                                          | Existing Farm Succession role                                  | Admission decision                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CAP intervention designs and planned amounts                | Discovery of possible national or regional financial support   | Use to find candidates; follow each to the managing authority and verify the current, usable support there. Do not publish the catalogue entry as an EU-wide aid.   |
| National or regional programme details                      | A dated, sourced record in the correct territorial edition     | Compare operator, purpose and coverage with existing resource IDs first. Update an existing record when it is the same scheme; add one only for a distinct service. |
| Examples and historical initiatives in external inventories | Operating succession services, land access, training or advice | Seek the primary operator's own page and its reuse permission. Verify operation, geography and who can actually use the service before making a lead or record.     |

The original operator's page is the evidence and access route in every case,
even when discovery starts with a licensed catalogue. Check its own terms for
both reuse and linking. If the operator forbids the link needed by a record,
do not add that operator, its URL, or its content to the research brief or
edition. A licence on a directory never grants rights over the operator's
pages, and an operator's permission does not override a directory's terms.

One checked example of duplicate avoidance: the original
[Generalitat de Catalunya succession-aid procedure](https://tramits.gencat.cat/ca/tramits/tramits-temes/24587-ajut-cooperacio-successio-explotacions-agraries?moda=1)
is already recorded as `cataluna-cooperacion-sucesion-transmision-conocimientos`
in the 2026 Catalonia edition. Its [own legal notice](https://tramits.gencat.cat/ca/ajuda/avis_legal/)
allows reuse of Generalitat-owned content under its open terms, subject to
attribution, the source's update date, faithful meaning and any specific
third-party rights. Both pages were checked on 2026-10-18. This example
confirms a primary, reusable access route and an existing record; it does not
justify a second record or a claim that the 2026 call remains open.

For each of ES, FR, DE, IT and PT: filter the Commission catalogue to that
Member State and relevant intervention types; record candidate identifiers only
after checking the original managing authority's source and reuse terms. Keep
national and regional eligibility at those levels. The EU layer carries the
research entry point, not five copies of national programmes. Other Member
States can be investigated by the same procedure when they enter the territory
catalogue with a complete edition and research brief.

This trial intentionally makes no finding that an intervention is currently
open. The catalogue itself says that its database follows approved CAP Strategic
Plans; an applicant needs the implementing authority's current page.
