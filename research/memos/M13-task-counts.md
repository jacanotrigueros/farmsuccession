# M13 — A count at the choice point must name its unit

17 October 2026; develop 88fc645. The published Castilla-La Mancha land task leads to
three references: clm-banco-tierras-disponibles and es-tierra-joven are announced/not
operating; es-red-terrae-banco-tierras is UNKNOWN. None is ACTIVE. This was checked in
the browser and in the data; no new source or status judgement was made.

Options:

1. Keep a total, explicitly called documented references. Preserves historical evidence
   and a simple number, but the reader still has to inspect availability one by one.
2. Keep the total and expose its state breakdown at the choice point. For this case:
   three documented references, two announced and one unconfirmed. Uses existing states,
   adds text, and still cannot equate ACTIVE with open applications.
3. Count only currently actionable resources. Attractive, but unsupported by one status:
   ACTIVE is existence, no-call services differ from grants, and 029 allows several
   independently answered procedures per record. A count of calls is not a count of
   resources, and a date-derived open call does not establish personal eligibility.

Recommend option 2, with the unit decided alongside task relevance. Do not hide records
or set a zero that erases announcements. Derive a disjoint record-state breakdown from
the same deduplicated set as the destination; if application periods are counted too,
name their unit separately and use 019/029 rather than inventing a summary verdict.

What would overturn it: observed comprehension showing a clear reference total is enough,
or a future data model establishing current actionability without guessing. No change
to 015, 019 or 029 is authorised by this memo.
