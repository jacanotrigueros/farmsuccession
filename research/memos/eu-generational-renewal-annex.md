# EU generational renewal inventory: five-country triage

18 October 2026. Research only; no change to the 2026 published edition or to
the territory catalogue.

## Source and rights

The European Union's [assessment of generational renewal strategies](https://eu-cap-network.ec.europa.eu/publications/assessment-generational-renewal-strategies-across-eu-member-states_en)
links **Annex II: Inventory of national and regional policy instruments** as an
XLSX file. Its introduction says the inventory includes both instruments
targeted at young farmers or generational renewal and general policies that may
also affect them. The study covers all 27 Member States and draws on earlier
periods as context. We inspected the supplied Annex II workbook, `Inventory`
sheet, 199 data rows, on 2026-10-18; the filename contains `20260223`. These
rows are study observations, not a list of currently open applications.

The publication links to the [European Commission legal notice](https://commission.europa.eu/legal-notice_en):
EU-owned website content is reusable under CC BY 4.0 unless a specific notice
applies. Attribute the European Union and the original work, indicate that
this is a summary and check third-party rights for individual materials. This
memo counts rows and paraphrases the source; it republishes neither the XLSX
nor its descriptions or third-party case material. Attribution: European
Union, _Assessment of generational renewal strategies across EU Member
States_, Annex II, publication URL above, accessed 2026-10-18; selection and
counts for the current five-country scope by Farm Succession.

The CAP intervention catalogue in `research/eu.json` and this annex have
different uses. The catalogue follows approved CAP Strategic Plans and helps
identify intervention designs. Annex II also includes non-CAP policy, fiscal,
land and advisory instruments; it is a research snapshot. Neither gives a
reader a verified live application route by itself.

## Admission and overlap checks

1. Filter to ES, FR, DE, IT or PT and classify each row as a possible service,
   financial support, policy, historical example or general measure. A law or
   broad strategy is not automatically a farmer-facing service.
2. Find the current page of the public authority or service operator. Check
   its own reuse and linking terms. If either prevents the intended use, keep
   the discovery URL in private notes only. Do not place its name, URL, facts
   or a rejection record in tracked research.
3. Verify territorial reach, operating state, access route and dated evidence
   at that original page. Do not infer an open call from an inventory row.
4. Compare the operator and instrument with the existing ID. Existing examples
   include a national training programme in ES, a regional succession aid in
   Catalonia and national land/finance leads already pending in IT. Add a
   genuinely separate instrument at its proper territory, or update the
   existing one with verified evidence. Do not multiply records because an
   inventory used a different name.

The [original Catalan succession-aid procedure](https://tramits.gencat.cat/ca/tramits/tramits-temes/24587-ajut-cooperacio-successio-explotacions-agraries?moda=1)
illustrates the gate: the [Generalitat's reuse notice](https://tramits.gencat.cat/ca/ajuda/avis_legal/)
permits reuse of its own content subject to attribution, update date and other
rights, and the procedure is already recorded as
`cataluna-cooperacion-sucesion-transmision-conocimientos`. Checked
2026-10-18; no second record is needed.
