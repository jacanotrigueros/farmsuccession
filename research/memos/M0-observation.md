# M0 — Audience evidence is still missing

17 October 2026; develop 88fc645. No Search Console or Bing query export was supplied
or accessed. Public search results establish that some documents are discoverable,
not who arrives or what they wanted. A query impression is not an arrival; clicks
are closer, and direct visits remain invisible under the privacy constraint.

Options: wait for query/page observations; infer demand from the corpus; or add
measurement on the site. The second mistakes supply for demand. The third violates
the settled privacy constraint and is not proposed.

Recommendation: retain the existing plan order provisionally, and review an export
of query, landing page, clicks and impressions with date range stated. Country and
device can help interpret it without adding a tag to the site. Keep anonymised or
suppressed queries visible as a limitation rather than interpreting their absence
as zero demand. Group by the question expressed: practical help versus documentary
research; a country name alone does not settle the distinction.

What would overturn it: actual query/landing-page evidence supporting a different
primary audience. No fixed query-count quota or fabricated observation is offered.
This is blocked input, not an engineering milestone completed by a public site: query.
