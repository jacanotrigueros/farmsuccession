# A measure links the records that apply it, as their sources say

## Question

The CAP support for young farmers is one European measure, and each country runs it its own way: a
national plan, then often a regional call. Spain's record of its CAP measures and the European record
said what the measure is, but neither led to the aid a reader can apply for where they live. Pages like
these looked empty. The same happened between a national programme and its regional versions, such as
France's AITA. How should a record point to the records that apply it?

## Evidence

On 24 November 2026, 44 of the 662 records named in a quote the higher measure they apply: its name (the
AITA), its plan (the PEPAC, the GLB-NSP, the PSN) or its intervention code in that plan (SRE01 in
thirteen Italian regions, 6961.1 in three Spanish ones, 75.01 overseas, C.2.2.1 in Portugal). Other
records mention the CAP without applying it: an advice service that helps fill in CAP forms, a portal
that lists every call. The only earlier link, a country file's `euCounterparts`, pointed from Spain's
side only, and at a record that itself linked nowhere.

## Decision

A record may give `implements`: the higher-layer record it applies, the words its own source uses for
that measure, and that source. The words must appear in a quote of that source, and the record pointed
at must belong to a file the record inherits: the European file, or its country's file for a region. A
regional aid under a country's plan points at the country's record when there is one, and at the
European record when there is none. Mentioning a measure is not applying it.

The measure's page lists the records that apply it, by country for a European measure, with each
country's regional applications under its national record, and by region for a national one. It says
that other applications may not be documented yet. Each record that applies a measure names it in its
facts, with a link. A European card on a country's page names the national record that applies it.
`euCounterparts` is removed: the link now lives on the record that makes the claim.

## Cost

A link counts only where the lower record's source names the measure, so many applications are not yet
linked: no Austrian record quotes its CAP plan, and most Spanish regional aids do not quote theirs. The
research prompt asks for the link while the page is open.

## Revisit when

The 2028–2034 CAP plans change how measures are numbered or shared out between levels, or readers show
that the list on a European measure is too long to use.
