# 050 — Prepare a country before its directory opens

**2026-11-26.** Add Romania's territorial and language infrastructure ahead of
its research passes.

The catalogue and edition checks still cover every prepared territory. A country
reaches the public build only when that edition contains a country file and at
least one resource recorded in its own national, hub or regional layer. EU
resources that apply there do not open a directory on their own. Selectors,
search facets and index filters use the resulting set of public countries, and
the sitemap follows the built routes. A country with an empty national layer
and documented regional resources still gets its full directory.

Romania uses the 41 counties and the municipality of București, grouped into the
eight development regions of Law 315/2004. The catalogue cites that partition,
HG 860/2016, the NUTS nomenclature and the AFIR exception. Călărași and Ialomița
stay in Sud-Muntenia for site navigation; AFIR directs both to CRFIR 8
București-Ilfov.

Romanian is the interface and edition language. The Hungarian names of Covasna,
Harghita and Mureș help search find resources filed at those leaves. Their
research briefs also seed Hungarian search queries. Article 94 of the
Administrative Code (OUG 57/2019) sets the minority-language threshold. Neither
an alias nor a research query makes a claim about the language a specific
operator offers.
