# 014 — A file's own voice may use the evidence it inherits

Decided 2026-10-02, milestone 05. Refines [005](005-a-figure-is-a-date-or-a-value-with-a-unit.md),
which decided how a figure is read but not whose evidence may support it.

## The question

A figure in prose must be supported by evidence, or the paraphrase becomes the one
unchecked claim in a document whose whole point is that claims are checked. The
first version of the rule read "evidence of the file", which is the obvious
reading and sounds unarguable.

It collides with the territorial model. Views are cumulative — EU, country, hub,
region — and a fact is recorded **once**, at the widest territory it applies to,
and never repeated lower down. So a region writing about the national aid it
inherits is describing evidence that, by design, is not in its own file. The
question is whether that sentence is unsupported, or whether the rule was asking
the wrong file.

## What the evidence said

Formentera. Its summary says the FOGAIBA establishment aid it inherits opens its
second 2026 call on 1 October, and the gate called that figure unsupported. The
quote is in the hub file for the Illes Balears, verbatim: _"Para la segunda
convocatoria, correspondiente al año 2026, el plazo de presentación de solicitudes
incluye desde el 1 de octubre de 2026 hasta el 30 de noviembre de 2026."_ The
sentence is not only supported, it is supported by the most specific evidence in
the whole edition.

So the rule left an author two ways out, both of them worse than the sentence it
refused: copy the national fact into the region's file, which the territorial model
forbids precisely so that one fact has one place; or write something vaguer than
what the source actually says.

Over the 101 files of the real corpus the figure rule produced seven findings. After
this change it produces six, and each of those six is a genuine defect — three in
Spain, three in Italy — so the one finding this removes was the only false positive
of its kind, and the kind was structural rather than accidental.

Two of the six are worth recording, because they are the opposite case and the rule
is right about them. A `limitations` entry in Castilla y León and in La Palma each
dated something the file deliberately does **not** publish: a one-off municipal
tender, and a land bank announced but not operating. Both dates existed only in the
research brief's own `reason` prose, with no quote behind them — one unverified
sentence citing another. A limitation carries no sources of its own, so the honest
place for a dated fact about something you are not publishing is a news item with
its source, written while the pass still has the page open. Afterwards it is too
late: adding a source read today to an edition published in September would re-date
a review nobody repeated.

## The decision

Two rules where there was one, because the two halves do not have the same
legitimate evidence.

- **A resource's own text** is checked against **its own** evidence, and nothing
  else. This is the strictest form and stays that way: a record describes one
  instrument, and a paraphrase that needs another record's evidence is a paraphrase
  about another instrument.
- **The file's own voice** — its summary, its limitations, its news — is checked
  against the evidence of the view a reader of that file has: this file plus the
  layers above it. Never a sibling region, another hub or another country, which are
  different territories whose evidence proves nothing here.

That containment is the same sentence as the lead rule of [010](010-the-brief-is-the-trace.md)
and the news-link rule, and it uses the same one implementation of the tree, so the
three cannot drift apart. The consequence is that the rule moved out of the
single-file checks and into the whole-edition checks, where the view exists —
[004](004-where-a-rule-lives.md) decides which of the two a rule belongs to, and a
rule that needs to know what a file inherits has only one possible home.

## What it costs

A reader following a figure in a region's summary may have to go one layer up to
find the quote, and the gate no longer promises otherwise.

The sharper cost is a coincidence it lets through. Figures are compared by value and
unit, not by meaning, so an unrelated inherited figure of 25.000 euros makes a
region's wrong 25.000 euros look supported. That was already true within one file;
widening the evidence widens the surface. The rule catches invention, not confusion,
and claiming more for it than that would be the mistake.

## What would overturn it

A real miss of the kind just described, found in real data: a wrong figure in a
file's own voice that an unrelated inherited figure covered. The answer then is not
to narrow the view again — that only brings back a false positive the model
guarantees — but to make support name its origin, so prose cites the record whose
evidence it rests on. That is a schema change, and a large one, which is why it
waits for a case rather than a worry.
