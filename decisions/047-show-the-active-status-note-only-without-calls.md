# 047 — Show the active status note only without calls

**2026-11-26.** Amends point 3 of
[042](042-answer-can-i-apply-under-the-title.md).

## The question

Does a record need the note under the active badge when it already answers
whether its documented periods are open?

## What the evidence said

At 375 CSS px, the published Aragón grant's answer began at 637 px. The
signpost above its title duplicated a distinction the dated panel already
makes. On the active La Estiva advice record the signpost explains the badge
without a call answer below it.

## The decision

Keep the quiet active badge and the individual dated answers on records with
documented calls. On active records without calls, retain the note explaining
the active badge.

## What it costs

The reader learns the difference between status and availability from the
dated answers on records with calls. The explanatory note stays available on
records where that context is absent.

## What would overturn it

Evidence that readers still mistake quiet active badges for open periods
when they can see the dated answer.
