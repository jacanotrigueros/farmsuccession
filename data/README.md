# Reusing the data

The [root licence](../LICENSE) covers the maintainer’s original descriptions and
licensable rights in the directory’s selection and arrangement, including any
sui generis database rights held by the maintainer. It does not make each JSON
file wholly CC BY 4.0: `sources[].quotes` in editions and research briefs are
third-party evidence. Source titles, names and underlying facts are not claimed
as original project expression; source databases may have separate rights.

Source URLs and `consultedOn` dates identify where and when evidence was read,
not its licence or permission status. No universal source permission is recorded
in the data. Check the original terms before reproducing protected material;
where necessary, contact the rights holder or responsible organisation directly.
Permission specifically to FarmSuccession does not automatically extend to you.
Lawful links, mentions, unprotected facts and uses allowed by a licence or law
remain unaffected. No extra restrictions are added to the project’s CC BY material.

Machine-readable fields and source quotations are unchanged by this clarification.
