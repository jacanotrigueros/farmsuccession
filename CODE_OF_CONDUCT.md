# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straight with people, and be straight about the evidence. Those are the same
rule here.

## What is expected

- **Disagree about the thing, not about the person.** "This threshold is wrong
  because the corpus says X" is the most useful sentence anybody can write here.
  "This is obviously wrong" is not.
- **Say what you verified and what you assumed.** Nobody is expected to be certain;
  everybody is expected to be clear about which is which.
- **Accept a correction, and give one plainly.** A mistake found early is cheaper
  than one found by a reader of the published data, so finding one is a
  contribution and not an accusation.
- **Assume the other person has read less of this than you have.** Most friction
  here is context nobody wrote down.

## What is not accepted

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or sustained disruption. Publishing
somebody's private information. Deliberately misrepresenting a source, a
measurement or what somebody else said — in a project whose entire value is that
its claims are traceable, that is the one form of bad faith with no way back.

## Scope

This applies in this repository and anywhere somebody is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Reports go to [contact@farmsuccession.eu](mailto:contact@farmsuccession.eu), the
project's own address, and are read privately by the maintainer. The maintainer may
edit, hide or remove contributions, and may block an account, explaining the reason
where it is appropriate to do so.

This is a single-maintainer project, which means two honest things: a report will
be read by one person, and it will not be read within the hour.
